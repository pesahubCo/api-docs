---
title: "Billpay Payments"
---

A **BillPay Control Number** is a dynamic number for a single transaction or invoice. It is ideal for one-time payments such as e-commerce orders, invoices, or single service charges.

For creating BillPay numbers, refer to the [API reference](https://docs.pesahub.co/api-reference/collection/create-a-bill).
