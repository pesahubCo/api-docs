---
title: "Billpay Payments"
---

A **BillPay Control Number** is a dynamic number for a single transaction or invoice. It is ideal for one-time payments such as e-commerce orders, invoices, or single service charges.

For creating BillPay numbers, refer to the [API reference](https://docs.pesahub.co/api-reference/collection/create-a-bill).

<Warning>
  BillPay is not available on sandbox. You must complete KYC verification and request access before using this feature.
</Warning>

## Enable BillPay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your Dashboard">
    Visit [pesahub.co](https://pesahub.co) and log in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC Verification">
    On the sidebar, go to **Settings \> Identity** to complete your verification.

    [Go to Identity Settings →](https://pesahub.co/app/settings/identity)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request BillPay Feature">
    Once your KYC is approved, go to **Settings \> Features** and request access to BillPay.

    [Go to Features Settings →](https://pesahub.co/app/settings/features)
  </Step>
</Steps>
